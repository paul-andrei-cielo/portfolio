Hello! I am Paul Andrei Cielo

but I prefer to be called by my second name, Andrei. I am a UI/UX designer and web and mobile developer who enjoys creating designs that are both visually appealing and functional. Aside from designing and developing websites and applications, I also enjoy creating digital art and graphic designs in my free time, as these allow me to explore my creativity and express my ideas in different ways.

As of now, I am continuously developing my skills and strengthening my foundation in both design and development. I believe that there is always something new to learn, especially in the ever-evolving field of technology. Through my projects and personal experiences, I hope to improve my craft, explore new ideas, and create digital experiences that are not only aesthetically pleasing but also meaningful, accessible, and easy to use.
